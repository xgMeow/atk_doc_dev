# Hold Segment

The Hold Segment is used to simulate a spacecraft's hovering operation, such as during landing, rendezvous, or on‑orbit station‑keeping phases, where the spacecraft maintains a fixed position relative to a specified reference frame. During this segment, the spacecraft's state is fixed in the hold coordinate system, i.e., constant position and zero velocity. The segment adds data points to the ephemeris at the specified step size until the defined stopping condition is met.
