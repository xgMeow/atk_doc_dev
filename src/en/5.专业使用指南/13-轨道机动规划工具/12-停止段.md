# Stop Segment

The Stop Segment is used to halt the entire Mission Control Sequence. When using this segment, enable it by checking the box <img src="../../../zh/5.专业使用指南/13-轨道机动规划工具/media/5.3轨道机动规划工具/image-28.png" alt="" no-view /> to the left of the **Enable** label. Once enabled, the entire Mission Control Sequence will stop and will not proceed further.
