# Globe Manager Access

The 3D terrain layer management module is used to configure imagery textures and elevation data for celestial bodies such as Earth, Jupiter, and Mars, enabling realistic surface appearance and terrain relief effects. It supports multi‑format data import, layer overlay, and dynamic control.

**Procedure:**

1. Click the menu: **View → 3D View** to open the 3D window.
2. Click **Globe Manager** to open the «3D Terrain Globe Manager» interface.
