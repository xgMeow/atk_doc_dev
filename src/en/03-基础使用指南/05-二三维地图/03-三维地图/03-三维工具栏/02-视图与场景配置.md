# View and Scene Configuration

## 3D Graphics Properties

Click the <kbd>Properties</kbd> button in the toolbar to open the [3D Graphics Properties] dialog, which allows fine‑grained scene configuration:

- **Detail Settings**: Adjust rendering precision for terrain and models to balance display quality and performance.
- **Lighting Settings**: Configure lighting parameters such as sunlight and self‑illumination of celestial bodies to simulate realistic space lighting.
- **Annotations and Labels**: Customize display styles for text annotations, lines, and markers in the scene.
- **Celestial Body Parameters**: Adjust the display of background bodies and starfield.

## 3D Snap

Click the <kbd>Snap</kbd> button in the toolbar to take a screenshot of the current 3D view:

- After capturing, the [Save As] dialog appears, allowing you to specify the save path, file name, and image format.
- Commonly used to save key simulation scenes, celestial attitudes, or orbital states for later analysis and reporting.

## 3D Record Movie

Click the <kbd>Record Movie</kbd> button in the toolbar to open the [Record 3D Movie] window for dynamic recording of the 3D scene:

- Supports configuration of recording parameters (resolution, frame rate, output format, etc.).
- Captures the full dynamic effects of celestial motion, orbital changes, and scene interactions during the simulation, generating video files for presentation or review.
