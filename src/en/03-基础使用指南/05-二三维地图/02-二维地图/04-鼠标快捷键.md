# Mouse Shortcuts

In addition to the toolbar, you can also control the view using mouse shortcuts for greater efficiency:

1. **Pan View**: Hold down the left mouse button and drag to pan the 2D view freely in any direction.
2. **Zoom View**: Scroll the mouse wheel – scroll up to zoom in, scroll down to zoom out.
3. **Context Menu**: Right‑click to open a context menu that supports:
   - Quick access to the [2D Property View] dialog.
   - Toggle the visibility of the 2D view toolbar.
