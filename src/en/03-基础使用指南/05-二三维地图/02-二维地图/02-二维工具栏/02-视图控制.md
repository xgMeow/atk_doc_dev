# View Controls

## Zoom and Reset

- **Zoom In**: Click the zoom in button to activate the function, then drag the left mouse button to select the target area for precise zoom‑in viewing of local details.
- **Zoom Out**: Click the zoom out button to step back to the previous zoom level, gradually returning to the default view.
- **One‑Click Reset**: Click the reset button to quickly restore the 2D view to the system default angle and display state, making it easy to reset the scene.

## Aspect Ratio Adjustment

Click the 「2:1 Aspect Ratio」 button to automatically adjust the view to a 2:1 aspect ratio, catering to visual presentation needs for specific scenarios.

## Setting the Central Body

Click the 「2D Graphics Window's Central Body」 button to customize and select a specific celestial body as the view background, flexibly adjusting the display reference.
