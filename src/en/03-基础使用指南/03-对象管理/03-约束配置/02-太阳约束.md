# Sun Constraints

## Function Description

Sun Constraints further filter access or coverage periods based on the relative geometry of the Sun, the Moon, the analysis object, and the associated object. Conditions such as Sun and lunar elevation angles, line-of-sight exclusion angles, and additional central body obstruction are used to retain periods that meet specified celestial-body geometry and light-avoidance requirements.

The individual Sun constraints are independent. A given time satisfies the Sun constraints only when all enabled conditions are met.

## Parameter Descriptions

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Sun Elevation Angle" desc="Limits the elevation angle of the Sun relative to the reference horizontal plane of the constrained object." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

The Sun elevation angle describes the apparent position of the Sun relative to the analysis object.
Select 【Min】 to retain periods when the Sun elevation angle is greater than or equal to the specified value. Select 【Max】 to retain periods when the angle is less than or equal to the specified value. When both are enabled, only periods when the Sun elevation angle is within the specified range are retained.

For example, setting Min to 10 and Max to 60 retains only periods when the Sun elevation angle is between 10° and 60°.

> **Note**: The Sun elevation angle indicates how far the Sun is above or below the reference horizontal plane. It is not the angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction. A positive elevation angle indicates that the Sun is above the reference horizontal plane; a negative value indicates that it is below the plane.

For the reference plane definition and calculation method, see [Sun and Lunar Elevation Angles](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#elevation).

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Sun Gnd Elev Angle" desc="Limits the elevation angle of the Sun in the local horizontal coordinate system of the reference ground object." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

The Sun Gnd Elev Angle is defined in the local horizontal coordinate system of a ground object, such as a Facility, and describes the elevation of the Sun direction above the local horizontal plane.
This constraint filters access periods according to the Sun elevation angle at a ground location. Its reference coordinate system is the local horizontal coordinate system of a ground object, such as a Facility.

Select 【Min】 or 【Max】 and enter the corresponding angle. For example, setting Max to -6 retains only periods when the Sun Gnd Elev Angle at the reference ground location is no greater than -6°. Setting Min to 10 retains only periods when this angle is no less than 10°.

> **Note**: When using this constraint, identify the ground object used as the reference for the calculation. The Sun elevation angle at a ground location must not be confused with the Sun elevation angle at the space-based platform itself.

For the coordinate system and angle definitions, see [Sun Gnd Elev Angle](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#sun-ground-elevation).

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Lunar Elevation Angle" desc="Limits the elevation angle of the Moon relative to the reference horizontal plane of the constrained object." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Use the same procedure as for 【Sun Elevation Angle】. Select Min or Max as needed and enter the angle. When both are enabled, only periods when the lunar elevation angle is within the specified range are retained.

> **Note**: This constraint limits how far the Moon is above or below the reference horizontal plane. To constrain the angle between the access line of sight and the Moon direction, use 【Lunar Exclusion Angle】 in the 【Line Of Sight】 group.

For the calculation method, see [Sun and Lunar Elevation Angles](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#elevation).

</Entry>

<Entry name="Additional Central Body Obstruction" desc="When enabled, also excludes periods when the access line of sight is obstructed by any assigned additional central body. This accounts for obstruction of the line-of-sight path by bodies such as the Moon." objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Select 【Use】 in this group. In the “Available Central Bodies” list on the left, select the bodies to consider and click the right arrow to move them to the “Assigned Central Bodies” list on the right.

During the calculation, the software checks for additional obstruction by the assigned bodies. If any assigned body obstructs the line-of-sight path between the access object and the associated object, the Additional Central Body Obstruction constraint is not satisfied at that time.

To remove a body, select it in “Assigned Central Bodies” and click the left arrow. Clear 【Use】 to disable the entire group of additional obstruction conditions. Bodies not added to the list on the right are not considered by this group.

> **Note**: This group determines whether a body obstructs the access path between the objects. It does not determine whether the access line of sight is sufficiently separated from the body direction or whether the target is illuminated by the Sun.

For the geometric principles, see [Additional Central Body Obstruction](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#central-body-obstruction).

</Entry>

### Line Of Sight

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Solar Exclusion Angle" desc="Sets the minimum allowable angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction." level="4" objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

For the general exclusion angle model, see [Line Of Sight Exclusion Angles](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#exclusion-angle). The body is the **Sun**, and the reference direction is the **line of sight**.
Select this option and enter an angle. If the actual angle between the access line of sight and the Sun direction is less than the specified value, the Solar Exclusion Angle constraint is not satisfied at that time.

</Entry>

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Lunar Exclusion Angle" desc="Sets the minimum allowable angle between the access line of sight and the Moon direction." level="4" objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

For the general exclusion angle model, see [Exclusion Angle Definition](#规避角定义). The body is the **Moon**, and the reference direction is the **line of sight**.
Select this option and enter an angle. If the actual angle between the access line of sight and the Moon direction is less than the specified value, the Lunar Exclusion Angle constraint is not satisfied at that time.

</Entry>

### Third Body Exclusion Angle

<Entry meta="Dimension: Angle|Unit: deg" name="Third Body Exclusion Angle" desc="Creates exclusion angle entries for specified celestial bodies to define angular conditions between the access line of sight and the direction to each body." level="4" objects="GroundVehicle,Sensor,Missile,Facility,Aircraft,CoverageDefinition,LaunchVehicle,SatelliteCollection,Ship,Satellite,SatelliteSystem">

Select 【Use】 in this group, click 【Add】 to create an exclusion entry, and specify the celestial body, angle, and Exclude setting. To remove an existing entry, select it and click 【Remove】. Click 【Remove All】 to clear all entries in this group. Clear 【Use】 to disable the group settings.

| Interface Item | Description |
|---|---|
| Celestial Body | The celestial body associated with the entry. |
| Angle (deg) | The angle threshold used by the entry. In the usual exclusion mode, this is the minimum allowable angle between the access line of sight and the body direction. |

> **Note**: Third Body Exclusion Angle and Additional Central Body Obstruction evaluate angular separation and path obstruction, respectively. Configuring one does not replace the other.

For the general angle model, see [Line Of Sight Exclusion Angles](../../../04-理论基础/约束配置/太阳约束.md#exclusion-angle).

</Entry>
