# From City Database

**From City Database** is a dedicated quick insertion method for Facility objects, supporting searches by address or city name with automatic retrieval of latitude and longitude coordinates, suitable for rapid construction of ground tracking and receiving station networks.

1. In the **【Insert Object】** window, select **Facility** and choose **「From City Database」** as the insertion method, then click the <kbd>Insert...</kbd> button;
2. In the pop-up window, search for the target city by name and select the corresponding data entry;
3. Set the display color for the facility;
4. Click <kbd>Insert</kbd> to complete the creation.
