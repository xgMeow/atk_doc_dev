# Select Countries and Regions

**Select Countries and Regions** is a dedicated quick creation method for AreaTarget objects, allowing direct generation of area targets based on administrative divisions, suitable for rapid modeling of scenarios such as national coverage and regional observation.

1. In the **【Insert Object】** window, select **Area Target** and choose **「Select Countries and Regions」** as the insertion method;
2. In the left panel of the interface, select the target **Country or Region**;
3. Configure in the right panel:
   - Insertion options
   - Insertion area range
   - Visual display color
4. After confirmation, the corresponding area target will be generated.
