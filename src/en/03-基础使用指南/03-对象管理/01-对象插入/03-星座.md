# Constellation

In the Insert menu, click the <img src="../../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/03-对象管理/01-对象插入/media/09-星座/update2601-32.png" alt="" no-view /> icon to open the **【Insert Constellation】** dialog.

From the drop-down list, you can select one of the following predefined constellations:

- BeiDou
- TIANLIAN
- GALILEO
- GLONASS
- GPS
- DSP

After selecting the desired constellation, click the <kbd>Insert</kbd> button to add the entire constellation in a single batch operation.
