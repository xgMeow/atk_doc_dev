# How to Use

## In the Coverage Tool

In the Coverage Tool, Figures of Merit are configured in the **Figure of Merit** area on the right side of the analysis window:

1. Click <kbd>Define…</kbd> to open the Figure of Merit configuration dialog.
2. Set the Figure of Merit type, statistical parameters, and satisfaction condition, etc.
3. After running the coverage computation, generate FOM‑related reports from the report output section.

For the complete workflow, refer to [Coverage Tool](../../../../5.专业使用指南/01-可见性与覆盖分析/02-覆盖性工具.md).

## In the Regional Coverage Tool

In the Regional Coverage Tool, Figures of Merit are managed as child objects of the **Coverage Definition** object:

1. Click <kbd>Insert</kbd>, select **Attached Objects → Figure of Merit**, then select **Insert Default Type** and click <kbd>Insert…</kbd>.
2. In the pop‑up window, select the parent **Coverage Definition** object.
3. Double‑click the **Figure of Merit** object to set the FOM type, statistical parameters, and satisfaction condition, etc.
4. After running the regional coverage computation, generate FOM‑related reports from the report output section.

::: warning Prerequisite for FOM Report Generation
Before generating Figure of Merit reports or charts, ensure that the corresponding FOM has been configured under the Coverage Definition object and that the regional coverage computation has been completed. If no FOM is configured, or if the coverage analysis result is empty, the related reports or charts may not produce valid data.
:::

For the complete workflow, refer to [Regional Coverage Analysis](../../../../5.专业使用指南/01-可见性与覆盖分析/03-区域覆盖分析.md).
