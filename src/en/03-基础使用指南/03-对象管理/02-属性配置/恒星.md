# Star

Star properties are mainly used to configure stellar targets in the celestial sphere background, and include two sections: **Basic Properties** and **Visualization**.

---

## Basic Properties

Defines the astronomical parameters of the star in the **J2000 Inertial** frame:

| Option | Description |
| ------ | ----------- |
| Right Ascension | Right Ascension of the star's position in the J2000 inertial frame, in deg |
| Declination | Declination of the star's position in the J2000 inertial frame, in deg |
| PM of Right Asc | Proper motion in right ascension, in arcsec/year |
| PM of Dec | Proper motion in declination, in arcsec/year |
| Magnitude | Stellar magnitude |
| Year Parallax | Annual parallax of the star |

---

## Visualization

- **Show**: Whether to display the star, whether to display labels, color settings.
- **Data Display**: Configuration is the same as in the 3D Graphics section of [Aircraft Property Settings](./飞机.md); not repeated here.
