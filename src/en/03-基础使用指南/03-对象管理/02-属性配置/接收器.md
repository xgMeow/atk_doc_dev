# Receiver

Receiver properties are used to configure signal receiving equipment on platforms such as satellites and ground stations, and include three modules: **Basic Properties, Constraints, and RF**.

---

## Basic Properties

Select receiver models of varying fidelity and configure the corresponding parameters:

- Simple Receiver Model
- Medium Receiver Model
- Complex Receiver Model

> - Simple/Medium/Complex models all support: **Model Specs, Modulator, Filter, Additional Gains and Losses**
> - Medium model **adds**: **System Noise Temperature** settings
> - Complex model **adds**: **Antenna Parameters + System Noise Temperature** settings

## Constraints

- Basic Constraints and Temporal Constraints: same as in [Aircraft Property Settings](./飞机.md)

- **Communications Constraints**: Supports metrics including Frequency, Rcvd Isotropic Power, Flux Density, EIRP, Doppler Shift, C/No, Power at Receiver Input, C/N, BER, Link Margin, Eb/No, Polarization Relative Angle, G/T, etc. All constraints support **reverse selection**.

RF environment settings are the same as those in the corresponding section of [Aircraft Property Settings](./飞机.md) and are not repeated here.
