# Missile

Missile property settings include: **Basic Properties, 2D Graphics, 3D Graphics, Constraints, and RF**.

- The overall configuration items are essentially the same as those for **Rocket**.
- The only difference is that **Swath settings are not included** in the **2D Graphics** section.
- All other parameters, interfaces, and operations are identical to those in [Rocket Property Settings](./火箭.md), so they are not repeated here.
