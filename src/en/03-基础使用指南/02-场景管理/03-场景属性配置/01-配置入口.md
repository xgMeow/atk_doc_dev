# Configuration Access

The scenario defines the basic properties of its objects. You can configure the **Basic** properties and **Visualization** properties of the scenario according to your requirements.

## How to Access

Right-click on the selected scenario and choose <kbd>Properties</kbd> to open the **Scenario Properties** window.
