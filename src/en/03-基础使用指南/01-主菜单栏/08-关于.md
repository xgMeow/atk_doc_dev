# About

The **About** menu contains three sub-items: Preferences, About ATK, and ATK Help.

![About Sub-Menu](../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/01-主菜单栏/media/08-关于/update2601-41.png)

## Preferences

Click the <img src="../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/01-主菜单栏/media/08-关于/update2601-42.png" alt="" no-view /> button to configure whether 2D and 3D views are enabled by default the next time a scenario is opened.

## About ATK

Click the <img src="../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/01-主菜单栏/media/08-关于/update2601-43.png" alt="" no-view /> button to view the ATK version information, as well as copyright and other product details.

## ATK Help

Click the <img src="../../../zh/03-基础使用指南/01-主菜单栏/media/08-关于/update2601-44.png" alt="" no-view /> button to open the ATK user documentation.
