# ATK Overview

As the foundational software in the aerospace engineering design software family, the aerospace mission analysis and design software is a core tool that spans the entire life cycle of space missions. Since 2020, with support from the China Manned Space Engineering Program, the manned space technology team at the National University of Defense Technology (NUDT) has leveraged over 30 years of technical expertise in this field to develop ATK (Aerospace Tool Kit), an independent industrial software for aerospace mission design. This effort has produced the first mature domestic software product capable of replacing STK. Currently, ATK is deployed in more than 300 organizations, effectively eliminating the serious security risks caused by long-term use of cracked versions of STK, **and has played a pioneering and demonstrative role in addressing the critical “bottleneck” problem in industrial software**.

ATK follows a fully independent and controllable development model, with five major module categories: standard platform, visibility and coverage, orbit design, mission analysis, and secondary development, comprehensively covering the basic functions of STK for aerospace mission analysis and design. In addition, ATK provides specialized feature modules for collision warning and avoidance, deviation analysis, and advanced constellation design, creating distinctive advantages in these areas.
