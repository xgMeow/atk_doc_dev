# ATK Overview

As a core software in the aerospace industry design software family, the aerospace mission analysis and design software is an essential tool throughout the entire life cycle of aerospace missions. Since 2020, with the support of the China Manned Space Engineering Program, the manned space technology team at the National University of Defense Technology has leveraged more than 30 years of technical expertise in this field to develop ATK (Aerospace Tool Kit), an independent aerospace mission design industrial software. ATK is the first mature domestic software product capable of replacing STK. It has been deployed and applied in more than 300 organizations, effectively eliminating the major security risks caused by long-term use of cracked STK, and has delivered a demonstrative impact in solving the "chokepoint" problem of industrial software.

ATK adopts a fully independent and controllable development model, covering five major modules: standard platform, visibility and coverage, orbit design, mission analysis, and secondary development. It comprehensively covers the basic functions of STK in aerospace mission analysis and design. In addition, the software has launched specialized modules such as collision warning and avoidance, deviation analysis, and advanced constellation design, forming distinctive features and advantages.
